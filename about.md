Elon Club is a registered non-profit association based in Prague. We bring together owners and fans of electric vehicles, Tesla and other brands alike. We share experience, give advice and help each other. Membership is free.

## What we do

- We run **Elon meetups** in Prague and exhibit at e-SALON and ElektroFest.
- We keep **guides** on choosing a Tesla, charging, insurance and Starlink.
- Through the **Cybertruckin.EU** project we are working to get the Tesla Cybertruck onto European roads.
- We run a **WhatsApp group** where members help each other every day.
