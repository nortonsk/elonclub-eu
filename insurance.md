## What to ask your insurer

- How the insured value of an electric car is calculated and how fast it depreciates.
- Whether the battery, charging cable and wallbox are covered by comprehensive insurance.
- What the roadside assistance limits are for towing with an empty battery.
- Whether repairs at an authorised Tesla service centre are allowed.
