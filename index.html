A club for electric vehicle owners and enthusiasts, Tesla and beyond.

We share experience, give advice and help each other. Based in Prague, open to EV drivers from all over Europe. Join our chat group or come to one of our meetups. Membership is free.

Meetups

Elon meetups take place in Prague, traditionally at Hotel Čertousy. EV talk, accessories, a light show, and above all people who drive electric every day.

Guides

How to choose a new or used Tesla, how to charge at home and on the road, what to watch out for with insurance, and how Starlink works.

Cybertruck to Europe

In January 2024 we ordered a Founders Edition Cybertruck. The goal of the Cybertruckin.EU project is to get the Cybertruck onto European roads.
