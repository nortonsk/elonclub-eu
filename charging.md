## Charging on the road

In the Czech Republic most routes are covered by the PRE, E.ON and ČEZ networks. Together with the operator PRE we were preparing a charging card that bundles charging across these networks. Current recommendations on charging cards are discussed in the [WhatsApp group]({{ site.cta.url }}).

## Charging at home

For home charging it is worth installing a wallbox at your house or parking space, or using a portable charging cable. More information (in Czech) at [autonabijecka.cz/domacnost](http://www.autonabijecka.cz/domacnost).

## Charging abroad

When travelling across Europe, the Tesla Supercharger network plus one or two roaming charging cards will get you almost anywhere. Members share country-by-country experience in the [WhatsApp group]({{ site.cta.url }}) and at [meetups]({{ rel }}meetups/).
