## Elon meetup

Come to one of the [Elon meetups]({{ rel }}meetups/), talk to current owners and ask anything about electric mobility.

Once you have decided, we can help you inspect a used Tesla or advise on everything else: insurance, financing, servicing, charging and so on.

## Buying a new Tesla

If you are going for a new Tesla, we recommend visiting the Tesla store in Průhonice near Prague and booking a [test drive](https://www.tesla.com/drive). The staff are happy to help and you can place the online order right there in the store. Directions to [Tesla Store Prague](https://www.tesla.com/findus/location/store/praguecz).
