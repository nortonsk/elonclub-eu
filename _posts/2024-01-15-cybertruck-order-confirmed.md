Ladies and gentlemen, we are thrilled to share some amazing news: we have ordered a Cybertruck! Yes, we managed to get a reservation for a Founders Edition Cybertruck and have now successfully placed the order. We are moving on to the next phase of the project: bringing the Cybertruck to the Czech Republic.

![Cybertruck order confirmation]({{ rel }}assets/img/cybertruck-order.png)

We would like to invite you to support the project and pick up a great reward in our campaign on the [Donio](https://www.donio.cz/tesla-cybertruck-v-cr) platform. If you would like to pre-book an experience ride in the Cybertruck and contribute to our success at the same time, have a look at the rewards available in the campaign. Your support means a lot to us and we are very grateful for it.

Thank you for your support. We are looking forward to the road ahead with our new Cybertruck!
